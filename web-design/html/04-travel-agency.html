Hong Kong

Hong Kong is a dynamic global metropolis where East meets West, famous for its towering skyscrapers, lush country parks, and legendary culinary scene. Spanning Hong Kong Island, the Kowloon Peninsula, and the New Territories, it offers an efficient, safe, and easily accessible gateway into Asia.

Packages

Hong Kong tour packages generally range from 3 to 6 days and feature popular highlights like Victoria Peak, Disneyland, and Lantau Island. Standard bundles often combine hotel stays with city bus tours or theme park tickets.
