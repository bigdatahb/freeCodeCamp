Yuan Longping

Father of Hybrid Rice

Here's a time line of Yuan Longping's life:

  • 1930 - born in Bejing China.
  • 1953 - Graduated from Southwest Agricultural College.
  • 1959–1961 - Experienced the Great Chinese Famine, which inspired his research into high-yield crops.
  • 1966 - Theoretical Breakthrough: He published findings on "male sterile rice" in the Chinese Science Bulletin, establishing the foundation for hybrid rice technology.
  • 1973 - successfully cultivated the first high-yield hybrid rice, boosting production and helping transform China from a food-deficient nation to a secure one.
  • 1991 - worked as the chief consultant for the FAO.
  • 2000 - won the State Preeminent Science and Technology Award of China2004 - won the Wolf Prize in Agriculture and the World Food Prize.
  • 2006 - he was a member of the Chinese Academy of Engineering, foreign associate of the U.S. National Academy of Sciences (2006) and the 2006 CPPCC.
  • 2011 - he was awarded the 2011 Mahathir Science Award.
  • 2017 - he became a laureate of the Asian Scientist 100 by the Asian Scientist.
  • 2021 - died at the age of 90.
